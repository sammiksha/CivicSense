# Dual Currency Deposit (DCD)

## How it works
A DCD pays an enhanced interest rate. In return the investor accepts that the deposit may be repaid in the alternate currency at a pre-agreed conversion rate if the exchange rate ends on the unfavourable side of that rate at maturity.

## Main risks
Conversion can happen at a rate worse than the market rate, so the investor may receive less value than the original deposit. The enhanced interest compensates only partly for a large currency move. The investor must be comfortable holding the alternate currency.

## Plain language
You earn extra interest, but if the currency moves against you, you may get paid back in another currency at a worse rate and end up with less.
