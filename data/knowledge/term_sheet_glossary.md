# Term Sheet Glossary (synonyms for extraction)

## Underlying synonyms
Reference asset, reference index, underlying, linked asset. NIFTY 50 may be written as Nifty, NSE Nifty 50, CNX Nifty. USDINR may be written as USD/INR or USD-INR.

## Barrier synonyms
Barrier, knock-in level, knock-in barrier, trigger level, protection barrier. Usually stated as a percentage of the initial or strike level.

## Coupon synonyms
Coupon, fixed coupon, interest rate, enhanced interest, yield. Quote it as an annual percentage. If written as a total over the tenor, convert to per annum.

## Notional synonyms
Notional, face value, investment amount, principal amount, deposit amount. Convert lakh (1,00,000) and crore (1,00,00,000) to plain rupees.

## Strike and conversion
Strike, strike level, initial level, conversion rate. Stated as a percentage of the initial level. For a DCD the conversion rate is the strike.

## Protection and participation
Capital protection, protected amount, principal protection: percentage of principal returned at maturity. Participation rate, upside participation: share of the underlying gain paid to the investor.

## Tenor synonyms
Tenor, maturity, term, investment period, expressed in months or years. Convert years to months.
