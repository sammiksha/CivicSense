# Capital Protected Note (CPN)

## How it works
A CPN returns a protected percentage of principal at maturity plus a participation share of any positive return in the underlying. With 100% protection and 80% participation, a 20% market rise gives a 16% return on principal.

## Main risks
Protection applies only at maturity and depends on the issuer being able to pay. Participation is below 100% and returns may be capped. Selling early can give less than the protected amount. If protection is below 100% some capital is at risk.

## Plain language
Your starting amount is protected if you hold to maturity, and you share in part of any market gains.
