# Regulatory and Governance Notes (illustrative, not legal advice)

## Suitability principle
Distributors and advisers are generally expected to assess whether a product fits the customer's risk profile, horizon and financial position, and to keep records. The exact rules depend on the product and the regulator, so a compliance officer must confirm the applicable circulars before production use.

## Record keeping
Each assessment stores the inputs, data window, rules fired, decision, explanation and model version so a reviewer can reproduce the result.

## Human oversight
The system supports the relationship manager; it does not approve a sale on its own. A REVIEW or MISMATCH result must be handled by a human.
