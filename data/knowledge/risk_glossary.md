# Risk Glossary

## Probability of loss
The share of simulated or historical outcomes where the investor ends with less money than invested. It describes how often, not how much.

## Barrier breach probability
The share of simulated paths where the underlying touches or falls below the barrier at any time before maturity.

## Fifth percentile outcome
In 5 out of 100 simulated outcomes the result is at or below this value. It is a bad but plausible case, not the worst case.

## Historical replay
Applying the product's payoff to every past window of the same length as the tenor. It shows what would have happened in real history, including crashes, but the future can differ.

## Monte Carlo
A computer simulation of thousands of possible future price paths using the volatility seen in history. A fixed random seed makes the result repeatable.

## Breaking point
The market fall at which the product stops being suitable for this customer, found by re-running the suitability rules under larger and larger market shocks.

## Concentration
Having too much of a portfolio tied to one underlying, so one market event hurts a lot.
