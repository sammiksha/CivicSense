INDICATIVE TERM SHEET - EQUITY LINKED NOTE
Issuer: Example Bank Ltd (illustrative)
Product: Equity Linked Note (ELN)
Reference Asset: NIFTY 50 Index
Notional: Rs. 10,00,000
Tenor: 12 months
Coupon: 12.0% p.a., paid at maturity
Knock-in Barrier: 70% of initial level (observed daily)
Strike: 100% of initial level
Capital Protection: None
Redemption: If the barrier is not breached, principal is returned. If breached and the
final level is below the strike, redemption = Notional x Final / Initial.
